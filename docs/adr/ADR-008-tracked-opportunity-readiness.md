# ADR-008: Tracked-candidate and pre-trade readiness persistence and supersession

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Pin one immutable TradePlan per symbol/session as a persisted tracked candidate with a controlled lifecycle, persist every analysis-time readiness assessment as an immutable history row, and never replace a tracked candidate in place. Feed/data-health suspension is a projection state, not a lifecycle terminal.

## Context

A.2.3b answers "what is happening with a found candidate opportunity now?". `trade-plan-v1` includes the current reference price in its entry zone, so a constantly replaced newest plan would keep price trivially inside a moving entry area. Readiness must therefore evaluate later market state against one pinned immutable `PlanId`, survive restart, and leave an auditable history for future outcome research, without mutating TradePlan or inferring the user's holdings.

## Decision

- `tracked_opportunity` persists the association/lifecycle. Selection (`tracked-opportunity-selection-v1`) pins the newest eligible `PlanDefined` plan only when the opportunity direction agrees, publication is Active, required current data is valid, risk and reward to Target 1 are positive, and no active tracked candidate exists for the symbol/session. Selection time is the analysis event time, so identical replay reproduces identical deterministic identity.
- While Active, mutable columns are exactly `Status`, `HasEstablishedEntryReady`, `TerminalAtUtc`, `TerminalReasonCode`, and `TerminalReason`. `Status` may move from `Active` to one terminal value (`Expired`, `Invalidated`, `TargetReachedBeforeEntry`, `ExtendedBeyondEntry`, `SessionEnded`, `PlanUnavailable`) and never back; `HasEstablishedEntryReady` is monotonic. Once terminal, every lifecycle field is immutable and only identical replay is accepted. All other columns are immutable and a conflicting write fails.
- A newer `PlanId`, a changed quality score, recalculated entry geometry, or price movement never supersedes an active tracked candidate. After a terminal condition, a subsequent evaluation may select the newest eligible candidate as a new row; the terminal row is never rewritten.
- `pre_trade_readiness_assessment` persists immutable assessments produced at analysis time (geometry from market state at `AsOfUtc` only). The PR #2 corrections advance the readiness definition to `pre-trade-readiness-v2`; historical v1 rows remain unchanged. Identical replay is idempotent; a different payload for an existing identity fails. `EvaluatedAtUtc` is excluded as a raw identity/material field, but controls validity expiry and current quote freshness; resulting state/reasons/evidence remain material.
- Entry confirmation derives the quote event time from `AsOfUtc - QuoteAge` and checks its age at `EvaluatedAtUtc`, inclusively up to `MaximumQuoteAgeSeconds`. Missing, negative, future, or over-age quotes cannot be EntryReady. Neither the snapshot nor its analytical quote age is rewritten.
- v2 always reports `CurrentSessionOnly`: the approved readiness rules do not consume historical levels. Merely attaching Available prior context does not justify `PriorSessionEnhanced`. That enum remains readable for historical records; future material use requires complete context and evidence identifying the consulted context/levels.
- On restart, active tracked candidates are restored and their plans resolved from the immutable `trade_plan` rows. Live startup and the existing one-second consumer health tick reconcile lifecycle independently of analysis batches, persist transitions, and evict ended candidates. Expiry is timestamped at the pinned plan's `ValidUntilUtc`, not the last market event or delayed processing time. Missing restored plans terminate conservatively. Price-driven terminal timestamps remain market-event time.
- A strictly later event-time observation in a different explicit session can end an older candidate at the first observed event time of that later session (or at earlier plan expiry). Session names are never sorted to infer chronology; equal-time ambiguity does not end candidates, and late older-session inputs cannot select a candidate or end the newer one. Without an authoritative live calendar, this is observed session transition, not an asserted exchange close. Recorded replay reconciles at event time instead of wall-clock time.
- Lifecycle-only reconciliation persists the association, not a fabricated market observation/readiness-history row. If a current analysis exists, its terminal projection uses the last known market state and explicit terminal evidence; future health changes preserve that terminal assessment. Active candidates alone have readiness re-derived on health changes (for example Suspended while the feed is unhealthy). Publication can still change independently.

## Consequences

- Readiness history supports future outcome research (which `PlanId` was followed, which states occurred, when, and on what evidence) without any position or execution state.
- Suspension never terminates or mutates a tracked candidate; recovery reevaluates the same `PlanId`.
- The approach boundary (`Scanner:PreTrade:ApproachDistanceRiskMultiple`, provisional development default 1.0, not market calibrated) is versioned through the scanner configuration; changing it requires a new configuration version, which participates in assessment identity.
- Projection-only re-derivations (health suspensions and lifecycle projections between analyses) are intentionally absent from readiness SQL history; lifecycle transitions themselves persist in `tracked_opportunity`.
